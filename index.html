Capabilities

Transient IR-drop, fast enough to stay in the loop.

Dynamic IR-drop is decided by the full time-domain response of a mesh with millions of nodes and thousands of time steps. GridStack replaces the repeated full-grid solve with a compact reduced-order model, so the analysis can run every iteration instead of once at the end.

11.9×peak speedup over a direct back-Euler transient solve

Built for sweeps

Domain decomposition

Partition the grid into balanced subdomains, solve each independently across multi-core CPUs and GPUs, and stitch the solution together at the shared boundary nodes — the full-grid answer, computed a piece at a time.
